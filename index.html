Remember / Recuerda:
Regular verbs: + -ed (watched, played, used). Irregular: read → read, swim → swam, write → wrote.
In the past, no -s for he / she / it: She played ✔ · She plays ✘.
Questions & negatives: did / didn’t + base verb: Did he swim? · He didn’t swim. Pasado: se añade -ed; no hay -s con he/she/it. Preguntas y negaciones: did / didn’t + verbo base.
